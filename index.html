§ 1. About

Research Profile

NA

I am a graduate researcher in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at North South University, working where computational fluid dynamics meets numerical analysis and scientific machine learning.

My thesis studies transport phenomena in porous media and nanoparticle-laden flows using finite-volume methods. Alongside it, I develop data-driven models — proper orthogonal decomposition and neural differential equations — that augment and accelerate classical solvers. An engineering background (B.S., Petroleum & Mining Engineering) keeps my work grounded in real physical systems.

Longer term, I want to bring tools from topological data analysis and differential topology to complex dynamical systems, building geometrically informed models that are robust beyond coordinate-based approaches.

Thesis & Selected Projects

M.S. Thesis · CurrentNorth South University · Expected 2026

Computational Fluid Dynamics of Transport Phenomena in Porous Media and Nanofluids

Finite-volume modelling of flow and transport through porous structures and nanoparticle suspensions, with attention to the numerical stability and convergence of the discretisation.

Supervisor: Dr. Preetom Nag, Associate Professor, North South University

Selected Projects

01.

Finite-Volume Flow Solver with POD Model Reduction

Finite-volume simulation of fluid flow, combined with proper orthogonal decomposition to build a fast reduced-order model of the dynamics.

Python FVM POD / ROM Navier–Stokes

02.

Neural ODE–PDE Hybrid Model of Traffic Flow

A neural ODE learns continuous-time density and velocity dynamics while the LWR conservation law acts as a physical prior; trained end-to-end through numerical solvers.

Python Neural ODEs LWR Model Conservation Laws

03.

Machine Learning for Building Energy Efficiency

Prediction of building energy efficiency from design parameters, with statistical benchmarking of competing model architectures.

Python Scikit-learn Regression

04.

Supercritical CO₂ Flow and Heat Transfer in Microtubes

Numerical investigation of heat transfer to supercritical carbon dioxide in microtubes, where strong property variation near the pseudo-critical point governs the flow.

CFD Heat Transfer Numerical Simulation

Machine Learning for Semiconductor Band Gaps
