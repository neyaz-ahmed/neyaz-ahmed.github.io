§ 1. About

Research Profile

NA

I am an M.S. student and Graduate Research Assistant in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at North South University, working in the Department of Mathematics & Physics on computational fluid dynamics and convective heat transfer.

My thesis compares the thermal storage performance of phase change materials (PCM) and nano-enhanced PCM in cavities with serrated heat sources, using transient melting simulations with the enthalpy–porosity formulation. My wider research combines finite-volume CFD with data-driven surrogate modelling for heat transfer in cavities, porous media, magnetohydrodynamic flows and geothermal systems; three manuscripts from this work are under review.

I came to applied mathematics from engineering (B.S., Petroleum & Mining Engineering, SUST), and I also write my own solvers, including an in-house D2Q9 lattice Boltzmann code in C.
